# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** created to analyze customer retention and identify the major factors associated with customer churn.

The project uses customer-level data, Pivot Tables, dashboards, KPIs, and business recommendations to understand **overall churn performance, contract type, internet service, payment method, and customer tenure**.

The analysis converts raw customer data into meaningful insights that can help businesses understand customer behavior and develop strategies to improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
* Identify the number of retained and churned customers.
* Analyze churn based on contract type.
* Compare churn across different internet services.
* Analyze customer churn according to tenure.
* Identify payment methods associated with higher churn.
* Identify important customer churn risk factors.
* Present key findings through an Excel dashboard.
* Provide recommendations for improving customer retention.

## 📑 Excel Workbook Structure

The workbook contains four main sheets:

### 1. Raw Data

Contains the original customer-level data used for the analysis.

It includes customer demographics, services, contract information, payment method, charges, tenure, and churn status.

### 2. Pivot Analysis

Contains Pivot Table-based analysis of customer churn.

The analysis includes:

* Contract vs Churn
* Internet Service vs Churn
* Tenure Group vs Churn
* Customer Churn Counts

These Pivot Tables help identify patterns and relationships between customer characteristics and churn.

### 3. Dashboard

Contains a visual **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** that presents the important findings in an easy-to-understand format.

The dashboard helps users quickly monitor customer churn and identify major churn-related factors.

### 4. KPI Dashboard

Contains the key insights, metrics, and recommendations derived from the analysis.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Contract Type & Churn

Customers with **Month-to-month contracts** show the highest churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.

| Contract       | Churned Customers |
| -------------- | ----------------: |
| Month-to-month |             1,655 |
| One year       |               166 |
| Two year       |                48 |

The analysis shows that long-term contracts are associated with substantially lower churn.

---

### 2. Internet Service & Churn

Internet service type also shows differences in customer churn.

| Internet Service    | Churned Customers |
| ------------------- | ----------------: |
| Fiber optic         |             1,297 |
| DSL                 |               459 |
| No Internet Service |               113 |

Fiber optic customers account for the largest number of churned customers in the dataset.

---

### 3. Customer Tenure & Churn

Customers in the early stages of their relationship with the company show higher churn risk.

The analysis highlights:

* **0–11 months:** 999 churned customers
* **12–23 months:** 309 churned customers
* Longer-tenure groups show comparatively fewer churned customers.

This indicates that the early customer lifecycle is an important period for retention efforts.

---

### 4. Payment Method & Churn

The KPI analysis identifies **Electronic Check** as an important churn-related payment category.

The workbook reports:

**1,071 churned customers** using Electronic Check.

This represents approximately **57.3% of all churned customers**.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project provides the following recommendations:

### 🔹 Improve Early Customer Engagement

Customers in their first year show higher churn. Businesses can focus on onboarding, customer support, and early engagement programs.

### 🔹 Encourage Long-Term Contracts

Month-to-month customers show considerably higher churn. Businesses can promote longer-term contracts through suitable discounts and loyalty benefits.

### 🔹 Review Fiber Optic Customer Experience

Since Fiber Optic customers account for the highest number of churned customers, service quality and customer support can be reviewed for this segment.

### 🔹 Promote Automatic Payment Methods

Businesses can encourage customers to use automatic payment options through suitable incentives and convenient payment experiences.

### 🔹 Monitor Churn Regularly

Regular monitoring of churn KPIs can help identify customer segments that require targeted retention efforts.
